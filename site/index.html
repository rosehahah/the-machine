THE MACHINE

"You are being watched. The government has a secret system…"

A fan-made, open-source recreation of The Machine from Person of Interest. Its point of view runs on your own Mac and iPhone cameras. It boxes everyone it sees, recognizes you as ADMIN, and issues numbers. Every box and label was checked against frames from all five seasons.

ENTER THE CONSOLE ↗ VIEW ON GITHUB

The console runs right here in your browser on a mock city of 500 people. Best on a desktop screen. Keys 1–6 switch pages.

[2] RUN IT ON YOUR OWN CAMERAS

What the MPOV does

  • Boxes every person on your Mac and iPhone cameras
  • Enroll yourself as ADMIN: yellow box, "GOOD EVENING, ADMIN."
  • Someone picks up a knife: white box with red corners, then THREAT
  • The Machine issues a number. It never says why.
  • Follows you from camera to camera

Quick start (macOS)

Needs uv and ffmpeg. The first run downloads the models and stock footage.

brew install uv ffmpeg
git clone https://github.com/rosehahah/the-machine
cd the-machine && ./run.sh

Faces stay on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
